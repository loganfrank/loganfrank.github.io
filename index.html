I am a third year PhD student in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at Ohio State University working as a graduate research associate in the Computer Vision Laboratory under Dr. Jim Davis. My main research interest lies in the area of generalizability and robustness of (convolutional) neural networks for vision-related tasks. I often utilize confidence and statistics, adversarial examples, and contrastive learning to accomplish my work.

Currently I am funded by the Air Force Research Laboratory (AFRL). Previously I was funded under a GTA position and worked with the Department of Food, Agricultural, and Biological Engineering at Ohio State.
