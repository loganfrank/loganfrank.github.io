I am a fifth year PhD student in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at Ohio State University working as a graduate research associate in the Computer Vision Laboratory under Dr. Jim Davis. My main research interest lies in the area of generalizability and robustness of (convolutional) neural networks for vision-related tasks. I often utilize adversarial examples, statistical methods, self-supervised learning to accomplish my work.

Previously I have been funded by the Air Force Research Laboratory Sensors Directorate (RYAT). Additionally, I have worked with the Department of Food, Agricultural, and Biological Engineering at Ohio State.
